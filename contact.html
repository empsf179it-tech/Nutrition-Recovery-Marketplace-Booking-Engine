Frequently Asked
Questions.

Find answers to common questions about our nutrition recovery services.

What is nutrition recovery?

Nutrition recovery focuses on replenishing essential nutrients and rehydrating the body after intensive workouts, treatments, or periods of stress.

How do I book a session?

You can book a session by selecting a provider from our Providers page, choosing an available time slot, and submitting a booking request with your details.

Are the providers certified?

Yes, all providers on the RECOVIA marketplace are fully vetted and certified professionals in nutrition, wellness, and physical recovery.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

Most sessions require a small deposit to secure your booking slot. The exact deposit amount is shown upfront during the booking process.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, you can cancel or reschedule your session up to 24 hours in advance. Please refer to our complete cancellation policy for more details.
